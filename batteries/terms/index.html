The transference number t+ is the fraction of the ionic conductivity carried by Li+; in a dilute electrolyte it is the ratio of the lithium diffusion coefficient to the sum of both. Chapter 9 · Ion transport
transient
During the pulse, lithium is taken from the surface faster than diffusion can refill it from inside, so the surface composition runs ahead and the voltage climbs: the transient change ΔEt=E3−E2 , measured after the immediate IR jump. Lab 3 · GITT

V

voltage efficiency
The voltage efficiency is the ratio of the average discharge voltage to the average charge voltage. Chapter 4 · Capacity and energy

W

working electrode
The working electrode is where the reaction of interest happens: an inert disc of glassy carbon, platinum or gold, polished before each run, or, in battery work, the composite electrode itself. Lab 2 · Cyclic voltammetry
