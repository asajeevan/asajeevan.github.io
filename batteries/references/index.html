How this course uses its sources. Every factual sentence in the course was checked against one of the textbooks and peer-reviewed articles below, all of which I have read in full; each entry says which chapters rest on it, and each figure’s “Source” toggle names the figure or equation it is drawn from. Where a source’s printed equation contains a typographical error, the course uses the form that follows from the source’s own premises. Analogies and my own opinions are marked as such.
