Battery basics, from zero

How batteries work

Follow one lithium ion through a cell: where the push comes from, how the ion travels, and why it sometimes gets stuck.

I spent my doctoral years building cells like the ones on this page. This is the explanation I wish I had had at the start: twelve short modules, each built around a figure you can play with. Every factual statement carries a numbered citation to a textbook or a peer-reviewed article, every curve is computed from the equation in its source, and every figure says what it is based on. Nothing here is experimental data.

“Just show me” keeps the questions, the figures and the one-line answers. “I want the equations” opens every “Go deeper” panel and links each module to its mathematics.
